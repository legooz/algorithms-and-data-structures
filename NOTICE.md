# Attribution

These are recovered student coursework submissions and working files maintained by Lars Goozen, University of Alaska Anchorage. CSCE A311 material dates to Spring 2024; CSCE A351 automata material dates to Fall 2025.

The notebooks incorporate classroom scaffolding and examples. Available files do not establish authorship of every line, so the repository does not present all source as solely student-authored. Existing code comments are preserved. Instructor-only tutorial notebooks and incomplete placeholders were omitted; some completed submissions still necessarily include starter implementations.

The demonstrative addresses, phone number and URLs in the automata sample paragraph are course example text. They are not the maintainer's contact information.

No repository-wide license is supplied because redistribution rights for incorporated course material have not been established. Original source hashes, selected cell indexes and publication transformations are recorded in PROVENANCE.json.

AI assistance was used to organize the recovered material, clear saved notebook outputs and metadata, and write documentation and validation checks in October 2026. Historical implementation logic was preserved.
